Example :

ETSY — Custom / Handmade Physical Goods Marketplace


PLATFORM TYPE: C2C / B2C physical goods marketplace (handmade, custom,
  vintage). Most comparable to Silaai's textile marketplace and custom
  alterations/garment orders.

  PAYMENT FLOW:
  - Buyer pays Etsy directly at checkout via Etsy Payments.
  - Etsy holds funds; seller does NOT receive money until Etsy processes payment.
  - Funds are released to the seller's Etsy Payments account after the order
    is completed and the standard processing period passes.
  - There is no hard "buyer confirmation" trigger for standard orders. The
    seller is paid on fulfillment + standard processing cycle, not on
    explicit buyer approval.

  KEY PROTECTION MECHANISM — PURCHASE PROTECTION PROGRAMME (PPP):
  - Etsy may cover buyer refunds up to $250 USD per eligible order.
  - Orders above $250 USD are NOT covered by PPP. For these, if a dispute
    is resolved in the buyer's favour, the seller bears full cost.
  - Buyers have up to 100 days after the estimated delivery date to open a case.
  - Before opening a case, there must be a 48-hour waiting window after the
    buyer first contacts the seller about the problem.
  - A case closes automatically when: (a) full refund is issued to buyer via
    Etsy Payments, or (b) buyer closes it as satisfied.

  ESCROW RELEASE TRIGGERS (IMPLICIT IN ETSY'S MODEL):
  Trigger 1: Order marked as shipped with valid tracking number
  Trigger 2: Estimated delivery date passes without dispute
  Trigger 3: Buyer confirms receipt (optional but can accelerate)
  Trigger 4: Case window (100 days) expires without a dispute being opened

  DISPUTE LOGIC:
  - If buyer opens a case: Etsy reviews. Seller must respond within 48 hours.
  - If Etsy rules in buyer's favour AND the order is PPP-eligible (≤$250):
    Etsy refunds buyer from its own funds, and seller keeps earnings.
  - If order is above $250: refund comes directly from the seller's Etsy
    Payments account or saved card. Seller bears loss.
  - Seller can appeal decisions using Etsy's appeals page.

  KEY INSIGHT FOR SILAAI:
  >> Etsy's model shows that custom/handmade sellers are HIGHLY VULNERABLE on
     high-value orders. The $250 coverage cap means expensive bridal couture
     or bulk orders have no platform-level protection. Silaai must define its
     own coverage threshold, especially since custom tailoring in India regularly
     exceeds INR 5,000–20,000+ per order. A tiered coverage model is needed.
  >> The 100-day dispute window is long and creates extended uncertainty for
     providers. Silaai should consider a shorter, defined dispute window linked
     to garment delivery + inspection (e.g., 7–14 days after confirmed receipt).
  >> Etsy's policy update in March 2026 narrowed late-delivery cases to "7+
     days after estimated delivery date" — signalling that over-generous buyer
     dispute windows create seller strain and platform abuse.
