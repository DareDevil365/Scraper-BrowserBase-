# Poggenpohl — Company Profile & Strategic Intelligence Dossier

## Target Customer Classification
- **Category:** **Ultra-Rich / Ultra-High-Net-Worth Individuals (UHNIs)**
- **Market Tier:** Heritage German Ultra-Luxury
- **Target Home Value:** ₹8.0 Crore to ₹50+ Crore (Luxury Estates & High-End Penthouses)
- **Income & Wealth Profile:** Business Elites, Multi-Generational Wealth (Net Worth > ₹30 Cr+)
- **Core Purchase Triggers:** Inventor of the fitted kitchen, iconic +MODO and +VENOVO architectural floating islands.

---

## 1. Corporate Identity & Manufacturing Footprint
- **Corporate Ownership:** Poggenpohl Group (World's oldest kitchen brand, founded in 1892; inventor of the modern fitted kitchen). Indian Partner: Plüsch Living.
- **Country of Origin:** Germany (Herford, North Rhine-Westphalia)
- **Indian Footprint & Experience Centres:** Plüsch flagship studios in Mumbai, Delhi, Bengaluru, Hyderabad, and Kochi.
- **Manufacturing Facilities:** 100% manufactured at Poggenpohl headquarters in Herford, Germany.
- **Quality & Environmental Certifications:** DGM Golden M, PEFC certification, ISO 9001/14001, German Design Award Gold.

- **Carcass Core Material:** Heavy-duty 19mm CARB-2 engineered core board with acoustic dust seals and polyurethane water-barrier edge seals.
- **Shutter Finishes Offered:** Multi-layer hand-polished polyester lacquer, anodized aluminum frames with smoked glass, volcanic ceramic, brushed stainless steel.
- **Hardware Partner & Systems:** Poggenpohl proprietary aluminum drawer sides with magnetic dividers and Blumotion dampers.
- **Manufacturer Warranty:** 10-Year International Poggenpohl Warranty.
