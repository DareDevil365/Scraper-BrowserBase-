## 2. Complete Pricing Architecture & Pricebook Breakdown

### A. Base Project Costs & Range
* **Minimum Base Starting Price:** **₹2,50,000 (Compact layout in Elina series)**
* **Typical Project Range (Mid to High):** **₹5,00,000 – ₹18,00,000 (Mid to high-end kitchens like Elegance, Myra, Luxe)**

### B. Indicative Rate Per Square Foot (Cabinetry & Shutters)
- **Elina (Poly-Laminate):** ₹2,200 – ₹3,000 / sq. ft.
- **Elegance (High Gloss Acrylic):** ₹3,200 – ₹4,500 / sq. ft.
- **Myra (Seamless Lacquer / PU):** ₹4,500 – ₹7,000 / sq. ft.
- **Luxe (Real Wood / Ceramic / Sintered Stone):** ₹6,800 – ₹11,000+ / sq. ft.

### C. Package Inclusions vs. Exclusions
* **Standard Inclusions:** European E1 cabinetry, 100% Austrian Blum hardware, LED lighting, cutlery trays, corner pull-outs.
* **Typical Exclusions (Add-on Costs):** Countertop granite/quartz, built-in chimney/hob (Würfel has tie-ups with Bosch, Hafele, Faber).
