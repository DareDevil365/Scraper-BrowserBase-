## 2. Complete Pricing Architecture & Pricebook Breakdown

### A. Base Project Costs & Range
* **Minimum Base Starting Price:** **₹2,00,000 (Essential package modular kitchen)**
* **Typical Project Range (Mid to High):** **₹2,40,000 – ₹6,50,000 (Kitchen package; Full 2BHK/3BHK: ₹4L – ₹14L)**
- **Package Tiers:** Structured into: 'Essential' (Budget friendly, ₹2L–₹2.5L base), 'Premium' (Best seller, ₹3.5L–₹4.5L base), and 'Luxe' (High-end finishes, ₹5L–₹8L base).

### B. Indicative Rate Per Square Foot (Cabinetry & Shutters)
- **Laminate on MDF/Ply:** ₹1,400 – ₹2,000 / sq. ft.
- **High Gloss Acrylic:** ₹2,100 – ₹3,000 / sq. ft.
- **PU Paint / Matte Finish:** ₹3,200 – ₹4,800 / sq. ft.
- **Glass & Veneer Elegance:** ₹4,800 – ₹7,200 / sq. ft.

### C. Package Inclusions vs. Exclusions
* **Standard Inclusions:** Base/wall cabinets, hardware, skirting, handles, free 3D design iterations, project management.
* **Typical Exclusions (Add-on Costs):** Countertops, civil masonry, chimney/hob, sink.
