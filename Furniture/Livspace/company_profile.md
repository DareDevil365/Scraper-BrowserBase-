# Livspace — Company Profile & Strategic Intelligence Dossier

## Target Customer Classification
- **Category:** **Middle Class to Upper Middle Class**
- **Market Tier:** Turnkey Full-Home Interior Giant
- **Target Home Value:** ₹50 Lakhs to ₹2.5 Crore (Apartment Renovations & New Buys)
- **Income & Wealth Profile:** Dual-Income Urban Couples, Tech Professionals (Annual Income: ₹18L – ₹45L)
- **Core Purchase Triggers:** One-stop turnkey solution (modular + civil + decor), 45-day guarantee, 10-year warranty.

---

## 1. Corporate Identity & Manufacturing Footprint
- **Corporate Ownership:** Home Interior Designs E-Commerce Pvt. Ltd. (Funded by KKR, Ingka Group/IKEA, Goldman Sachs, Venturi Partners).
- **Country of Origin:** India (Bengaluru / Singapore)
- **Indian Footprint & Experience Centres:** Present in 50+ cities across India with 60+ Experience Centres (Bengaluru, Mumbai, Delhi-NCR, Hyderabad, Chennai, Pune, Kolkata, etc.).
- **Manufacturing Facilities:** Network of contracted OEM modular factories operating proprietary Canvas design and manufacturing execution software.
- **Quality & Environmental Certifications:** ISO 9001/14001, CARB-2 compliant boards, DuraBuild technology certified.

## 2. Product Benchmarking: Substrates, Hardware & Warranty
- **Carcass Core Material:** IS 710 BWP Marine Plywood, BWR Plywood, and High Density Moisture Resistant (HDHMR) board with DuraBuild technology.
- **Shutter Finishes Offered:** Anti-bubble laminate, acrylic, PU lacquer, membrane, and aluminum-profile lacquered glass.
- **Hardware Partner & Systems:** Hettich, Blum, and Livspace-approved European hardware partners.
- **Manufacturer Warranty:** Flat 10-Year Warranty on modular woodwork; 1-year service warranty.
