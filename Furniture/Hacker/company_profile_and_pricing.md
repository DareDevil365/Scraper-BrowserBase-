## 2. Complete Pricing Architecture & Pricebook Breakdown

### A. Base Project Costs & Range
* **Minimum Base Starting Price:** **₹4,50,000 (concept130 series compact layout)**
* **Typical Project Range (Mid to High):** **₹10,00,000 – ₹35,00,000+ (systemat series luxury island setup)**
- **Price Group Structure:** Organized into two core lines: 'concept130' (value-engineered German luxury PG 1-6) and 'systemat' (bespoke architectural grid system PG 1-10).

### B. Indicative Rate Per Square Foot (Cabinetry & Shutters)
- **concept130 Melamine / Textured (PG 1-2):** ₹3,000 – ₹4,200 / sq. ft.
- **concept130 PerfectSoft / Anti-fingerprint (PG 3-4):** ₹4,500 – ₹6,000 / sq. ft.
- **systemat Satin Lacquer / Acrylic (PG 5-6):** ₹6,000 – ₹9,000 / sq. ft.
- **systemat Real Wood Veneer / Ceramic / Glass (PG 7-10):** ₹8,500 – ₹15,000+ / sq. ft.

### C. Package Inclusions vs. Exclusions
* **Standard Inclusions:** Laser-welded PUR water-resistant carcass, Grass Nova Pro Scala drawer systems, anti-slip mats, LED ambient profiles.
* **Typical Exclusions (Add-on Costs):** Appliances (Miele, Siemens, Gaggenau), countertop fabrication, civil plumbing.
