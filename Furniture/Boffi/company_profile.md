# Boffi — Company Profile & Strategic Intelligence Dossier

- **Category:** **Ultra-Rich / Ultra-High-Net-Worth Individuals (UHNIs)**

- **Target Home Value:** ₹20.0 Crore to ₹200+ Crore (Bespoke Mansions, Sea-Facing Estates)

- **Core Purchase Triggers:** Piero Lissoni architectural purism, monolithic marble & steel, absolute exclusivity.

## 1. Corporate Identity & Manufacturing Footprint
- **Corporate Ownership:** Boffi | De Padova Group (Art direction by legendary architect Piero Lissoni; the global benchmark for minimalist architectural luxury).
- **Country of Origin:** Italy (Lentate sul Seveso, Monza)
- **Indian Footprint & Experience Centres:** Dedicated Boffi mono-brand flagship studios in Mumbai (Dr. E. Moses Road, Worli) and New Delhi.
- **Manufacturing Facilities:** 100% handcrafted and engineered in Lentate sul Seveso, Lombardy, Italy.
- **Quality & Environmental Certifications:** Compasso d'Oro Career Award, ISO 9001/14001, OHSAS 18001.

- **Carcass Core Material:** Heavy-duty CARB-2 marine composite board and solid brushed stainless steel frameworks.

- **Hardware Partner & Systems:** Exclusively engineered Boffi concealed sliding and pivot hardware with hydraulic dampers.
