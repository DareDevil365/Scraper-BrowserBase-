# Poliform — Company Profile & Strategic Intelligence Dossier

## Target Customer Classification
- **Category:** **Ultra-Rich / Ultra-High-Net-Worth Individuals (UHNIs)**
- **Market Tier:** Ultra-Luxury Billionaire & Penthouse Class
- **Target Home Value:** ₹15.0 Crore to ₹150+ Crore (Penthouses, Mansions, Farmhouses)
- **Income & Wealth Profile:** Industrialists, Promoters, Top Celebrities (Net Worth > ₹50 Cr – ₹500+ Cr)
- **Core Purchase Triggers:** Architectural minimalism, bespoke Italian prestige, motorized pocket concealment.

---

## 1. Corporate Identity & Manufacturing Footprint
- **Corporate Ownership:** Poliform S.p.A. (Family owned Italian ultra-luxury design house, operating since 1970).
- **Country of Origin:** Italy (Inverigo, Como)
- **Indian Footprint & Experience Centres:** Flagship multi-story mono-brand showrooms in Mumbai (Mahalaxmi / Lower Parel), New Delhi (MG Road), and Bengaluru (Lavelle Road) in partnership with luxury importers.
- **Manufacturing Facilities:** 100% handcrafted and automated in Inverigo and Brianza, Italy.
- **Quality & Environmental Certifications:** FSC timber certification, CARB-2 California Air Resources Board formaldehyde compliance, ISO 14001.

---

## 2. Product Benchmarking: Substrates, Hardware & Warranty
- **Carcass Core Material:** Exclusive Italian water-repellent CARB-2 particleboard with antibacterial Melamine resin treatment and solid wood framing.
- **Shutter Finishes Offered:** Embossed and glossy lacquers, brushed metals (bronze, brass, steel), Spessart oak, Canaletto walnut, sintered stone.
- **Hardware Partner & Systems:** Custom-engineered Blum Legrabox mechanisms finished in anthracite with magnetic dampers and invisible touch latches.
- **Manufacturer Warranty:** 5-year comprehensive international warranty with dedicated white-glove maintenance.

---

## 3. Go-to-Market Channels, Lead Times & Service SLA
- **Distribution Channels:** Direct architectural commissions, high-end interior designers, exclusive Poliform mono-brand flagship stores.
- **Delivery & Installation Lead Time:** 14 to 18 weeks manufacturing and ocean shipping timeline from Italy. White-glove installation supervised by Italian-trained technicians.
- **Digital Capabilities:** 3D Design simulation, virtual experience center walkthroughs, and custom Bill of Quantities (BOQ) configurators.

---

## 4. Strategic Positioning & Market Role
- **Positioning:** Fits into **Tier 1: Largest Scale, Strongest Traction (Ultra-Luxury Icon)**.
- **Key Competitive Edge:** Direct access to Italy (Inverigo, Como) engineering standards, certified low-emission materials, and dedicated service infrastructure in India.
