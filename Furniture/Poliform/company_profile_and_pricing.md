## 1. Company Snapshot & Manufacturing Footprint
* **Corporate Ownership:** Poliform S.p.A. (Family owned Italian ultra-luxury design house, operating since 1970).
* **Indian Presence & Channel Footprint:** Flagship multi-story mono-brand showrooms in Mumbai (Mahalaxmi / Lower Parel), New Delhi (Mehrauli-Gurgaon Road), and Bengaluru (Lavelle Road) in partnership with luxury importers (Ventura Interiors).
* **Manufacturing Facilities:** 100% handcrafted and automated in Inverigo and Brianza, Italy.
* **Quality & Environmental Certifications:** FSC timber certification, CARB-2 California Air Resources Board formaldehyde compliance, ISO 14001.
