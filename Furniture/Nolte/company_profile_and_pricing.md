## 2. Complete Pricing Architecture & Pricebook Breakdown

### A. Base Project Costs & Range
* **Minimum Base Starting Price:** **₹3,50,000 (Compact German layout in PG 1-2)**
* **Typical Project Range (Mid to High):** **₹7,50,000 – ₹24,00,000 (Matrix 150 grid with island and MatrixArt handleless)**
- **Price Group Structure:** Strict 8-tier price group matrix (PG 1 to PG 8). Uses the famous 150mm 'Matrix 150' and 'Matrix 900' height raster systems.

### B. Indicative Rate Per Square Foot (Cabinetry & Shutters)
- **Melamine / Feel Matte (PG 1-2):** ₹2,600 – ₹3,600 / sq. ft.
- **Soft-Lack / Plus Anti-fingerprint (PG 3-4):** ₹3,800 – ₹5,200 / sq. ft.
- **Nova Lack / Alpha Gloss Lacquer (PG 5-6):** ₹5,200 – ₹7,800 / sq. ft.
- **Corona Glass / Legno Real Wood / Cement (PG 7-8):** ₹7,500 – ₹13,000+ / sq. ft.

### C. Package Inclusions vs. Exclusions
* **Standard Inclusions:** Complete German carcass, front shutters, sealed edging, PremiumLINE drawer boxes, dust-seal profiles.
* **Typical Exclusions (Add-on Costs):** Countertop quartz, built-in appliances, sink/plumbing.
