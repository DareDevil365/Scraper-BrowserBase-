## 2. Project-Level Pricing

> TRANSPARENCY DISCLOSURE: NoBroker article (meta: 'price ranges from Rs. 2,500 to...') provides researched Hafele market rates. Hafele is hardware-focused; kitchen cost depends heavily on hardware selection.
