## 2. Complete Pricing Architecture & Pricebook Breakdown

### A. Base Project Costs & Range
* **Minimum Base Starting Price:** **₹2,50,000 (Modular kitchen using Häfele hardware & standard cabinets)**
* **Typical Project Range (Mid to High):** **₹5,00,000 – ₹15,00,000 (Complete kitchen including premium fittings)**
- **Hardware Cost Dynamics:** Hardware typically constitutes 40-45% of the total budget. MatrixBox slim drawer systems retail at ₹4,200 – ₹8,500 per drawer; Metalla hinges at ₹220 – ₹550 per pair; Tall pantry units at ₹35,000 – ₹1,20,000.

### B. Indicative Rate Per Square Foot (Cabinetry & Shutters)
- **Standard Laminate on BWR Ply:** ₹2,200 – ₹3,200 / sq. ft.
- **High Gloss Acrylic on HDHMR:** ₹3,200 – ₹4,500 / sq. ft.
- **PU Lacquer / Suede Finish:** ₹4,500 – ₹7,000 / sq. ft.
- **Glass & Veneer Elegance:** ₹6,500 – ₹10,000+ / sq. ft.

### C. Package Inclusions vs. Exclusions
* **Standard Inclusions:** Häfele MatrixBox / Grass drawers, soft-close hinges, cutlery trays, corner carousels, dustbin pullouts, Loox 12V LED lighting.
* **Typical Exclusions (Add-on Costs):** Appliances (Asko, Bosch, or Häfele Appliances sold separately), stone countertops, civil work.
