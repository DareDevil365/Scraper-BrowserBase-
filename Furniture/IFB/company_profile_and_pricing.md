## 2. Complete Pricing Architecture & Pricebook Breakdown

### A. Base Project Costs & Range
* **Minimum Base Starting Price:** **₹1,55,000 (Model: Bohemian Rhapsody straight modular kitchen)**
* **Typical Project Range (Mid to High):** **₹2,50,000 – ₹6,00,000 (Models like Retro Revival @ ₹2.85L, Everyday Elegance @ ₹4.99L)**

### B. Indicative Rate Per Square Foot (Cabinetry & Shutters)
- **Matte & Textured Laminate:** ₹1,400 – ₹2,000 / sq. ft.
- **High Gloss Acrylic:** ₹2,200 – ₹3,200 / sq. ft.
- **PU Painted Finish:** ₹3,200 – ₹4,800 / sq. ft.
- **Glass & Veneer Premium:** ₹5,000 – ₹7,500 / sq. ft.

### C. Package Inclusions vs. Exclusions
* **Standard Inclusions:** Base and wall cabinetry, internal wire baskets, soft-close hinges, skirting, plinth feet, cutlery tray, and bundle discounts on IFB chimneys and hobs.
* **Typical Exclusions (Add-on Costs):** Granite/Quartz slab, installation of non-IFB appliances, civil masonry.
