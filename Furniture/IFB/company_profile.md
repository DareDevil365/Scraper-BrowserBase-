# IFB Modular Kitchens — Company Profile & Strategic Intelligence Dossier

## Target Customer Classification
- **Category:** **Upper Middle Class (Mass Affluent)**
- **Market Tier:** Turnkey Domestic Appliance & Kitchen Integrator
- **Target Home Value:** ₹70 Lakhs to ₹2.5 Crore (Mid & High-Rise Apartments)
- **Income & Wealth Profile:** Corporate Salaried, Doctors, PSU Officers (Annual Income: ₹25L – ₹60L)
- **Core Purchase Triggers:** Seamless appliance-cabinet integration, trusted Indian engineering, 45-day SLA.

---

## 1. Corporate Identity & Manufacturing Footprint
- **Corporate Ownership:** IFB Industries Ltd. (Publicly traded consumer durable giant with over ₹4,000 Cr revenue).
- **Country of Origin:** India (Kolkata / Goa / Bengaluru)
- **Indian Footprint & Experience Centres:** Exclusive IFB Point modular kitchen experience centers across Bengaluru, Goa, Mumbai, Kolkata, Hyderabad, and Chennai.
- **Manufacturing Facilities:** State-of-the-art automated woodworking and sheet metal manufacturing plant in Verna, Goa.
- **Quality & Environmental Certifications:** ISO 9001:2015, E1 emission compliance, boiling water resistance tested per IS 710 standards.

---

## 2. Product Benchmarking: Substrates, Hardware & Warranty
- **Carcass Core Material:** Boiling Water Proof (BWP) Marine Ply grade IS 710 and Moisture Resistant High Density Fiberboard.
- **Shutter Finishes Offered:** Anti-scratch acrylic, high-pressure laminate, membrane pressed, and PU lacquer.
- **Hardware Partner & Systems:** Hettich Sensys / Häfele soft close drawer channels and concealed hinges.
- **Manufacturer Warranty:** 10-Year comprehensive warranty on cabinetry; 5-year warranty on appliances.

---

## 3. Go-to-Market Channels, Lead Times & Service SLA
- **Distribution Channels:** IFB Point retail network, official IFB website, direct home consultation via 3D designers.
- **Delivery & Installation Lead Time:** Guaranteed 45-day installation timeline from final design sign-off. Free initial design consultation and no-cost EMI options.
- **Digital Capabilities:** 3D Design simulation, virtual experience center walkthroughs, and custom Bill of Quantities (BOQ) configurators.

---

## 4. Strategic Positioning & Market Role
- **Positioning:** Fits into **Tier 1: Largest Scale, Strongest Traction (Domestic Turnkey Appliance Integrator)**.
- **Key Competitive Edge:** Direct access to India (Kolkata / Goa / Bengaluru) engineering standards, certified low-emission materials, and dedicated service infrastructure in India.
