# Itallica — Company Profile & Strategic Intelligence Dossier

## Target Customer Classification
- **Category:** **Budget to Value Home Renovators**
- **Market Tier:** Contractor & Trade Marketplace Network
- **Target Home Value:** ₹25 Lakhs to ₹90 Lakhs (Affordable & Rental Apartments)
- **Income & Wealth Profile:** Budget Homeowners, Rental Landlords (Annual Income: ₹8L – ₹20L)
- **Core Purchase Triggers:** Lowest upfront price per square foot, basic functional wire baskets, quick installation.

---

## 1. Corporate Identity & Manufacturing Footprint
- **Corporate Ownership:** Italica Modular Furniture / Trade Network fabricators.
- **Country of Origin:** India (National Trade Network)
- **Indian Footprint & Experience Centres:** Presence across dealer showrooms, building material distributors, and trade portals in Delhi-NCR, Maharashtra, and Gujarat.
- **Manufacturing Facilities:** Decentralized regional modular fabrication units using automated edgebanding machines.
- **Quality & Environmental Certifications:** Commercial BWR compliance, standard commercial warranties.

---

## 2. Product Benchmarking: Substrates, Hardware & Warranty
- **Carcass Core Material:** Commercial Boiling Water Resistant (BWR) plywood and melamine-faced particleboard.
- **Shutter Finishes Offered:** 0.8mm / 1mm laminate, membrane foil, and high-gloss PVC laminate.
- **Hardware Partner & Systems:** Local and imported soft-close hinges, telescopic drawer channels.
- **Manufacturer Warranty:** 3 to 5 Years standard warranty against manufacturing defects.

---

## 3. Go-to-Market Channels, Lead Times & Service SLA

- **Delivery & Installation Lead Time:** 20 to 30 days fast-track turnaround.
