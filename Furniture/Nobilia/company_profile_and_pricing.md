## 1. Company Snapshot & Manufacturing Footprint
* **Corporate Ownership:** Nobilia-Werke J. Stickling GmbH & Co. KG (Privately held, Europe's largest kitchen manufacturer with >€1.6 Billion annual revenue)
* **Indian Presence & Channel Footprint:** Joint Venture & Master Franchise partnership with Prism Johnson Limited (H & R Johnson division). Operates 35+ experience centers across Mumbai, Delhi NCR, Bengaluru, Hyderabad, Chennai, Ahmedabad, Pune, Kolkata, and Kochi.

## 2. Complete Pricing Architecture & Pricebook Breakdown

### A. Base Project Costs & Range
* **Minimum Base Starting Price:** **₹3,50,000 (Compact straight/L-shaped basic layout)**
* **Typical Project Range (Mid to High):** **₹8,00,000 – ₹25,00,000+ (depending on finishes and appliances)**
- **Price Group Structure:** Uses strict German Price Groups PG 1 to PG 9. PG 1 (Speed, Laser) is entry melamine; PG 4 (Touch, Easytouch anti-fingerprint) is mid; PG 9 (Artis, StoneArt) is luxury.

### B. Indicative Rate Per Square Foot (Cabinetry & Shutters)
- **Laminate / Melamine (PG 1-2):** ₹2,800 – ₹3,800 / sq. ft.
- **High Pressure Laminate / Fenix Matte (PG 3-4):** ₹4,000 – ₹5,500 / sq. ft.
- **High Gloss Acrylic / Polymer Glass (PG 5-6):** ₹5,500 – ₹8,000 / sq. ft.
- **Seamless Deep Lacquer / PU (PG 7-8):** ₹8,000 – ₹11,000 / sq. ft.
- **Real Wood Veneer / Ceramic / Stone (PG 9):** ₹11,000 – ₹16,000+ / sq. ft.

### C. Package Inclusions vs. Exclusions
* **Standard Inclusions:** Factory-assembled carcasses, front shutters, integrated plinths, soft-close hardware, internal organizational trays, cutlery inserts.
* **Typical Exclusions (Add-on Costs):** Countertops (usually sourced locally in quartz/granite via Prism Johnson), appliances (built-in Bosch, Siemens, Miele), sink/faucet, civil & plumbing modifications.
