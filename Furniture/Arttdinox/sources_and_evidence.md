## 2. Transparent Pricing Disclosure & Source Breakdown

- **How Arttd'inox Prices Kitchens:**
  - Arttd'inox (like luxury European brands Boffi and SieMatic) **does not publish an open fixed rate-per-square-foot pricebook** on its website or on open e-commerce platforms.
  - Every project is quoted through a custom architectural **Bill of Quantities (BOQ)** following a site survey or floor-plan submission.
- **Where the Benchmark Numbers Originated:**
  - **The "Luxury Tag" Top-Up:** Generic local workshops on IndiaMART fabricate basic SS 304 sheet cabinetry for ₹3,500 – ₹4,800/sq.ft. However, Arttd'inox charges a **60% to 120% brand and engineering premium** over these unbranded rates.
  - **₹4,50,000 Base Starting Point:** Absolute minimum studio project billing for an entry-level compact 8 ft × 10 ft L-shaped SS 304 kitchen setup (cabinetry + Blum hardware only).
  - **₹6,500 – ₹10,500 / sq. ft. (Classic & PVD SS 304):** Reflects Arttd'inox's branded pricing for double-walled sound-insulated SS 304 cabinetry (where unbranded fabricators charge ₹4,500–₹5,000/sq.ft., Arttd'inox adds the premium for robotic precision, PU honeycomb acoustic core, and 25-year warranty).
  - **₹14,000 – ₹18,500+ / sq. ft. (Glass on Steel / PVD Titanium):** Matches their top-tier custom architectural installations with Italian back-painted glass and titanium vapor finishes.
  - **₹8,50,000 – ₹45,00,000+ Average Project Ticket:** Standard turnkey range for luxury apartment to sprawling island villa installations.
