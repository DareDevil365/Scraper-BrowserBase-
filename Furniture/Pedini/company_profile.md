# Pedini — Company Profile & Strategic Intelligence Dossier

## Target Customer Classification
- **Category:** **Ultra-Rich / Ultra-High-Net-Worth Individuals (UHNIs)**
- **Market Tier:** Bespoke Italian Curved Architecture (Direct Import)
- **Target Home Value:** ₹12.0 Crore to ₹80+ Crore (Architectural Masterpieces)
- **Income & Wealth Profile:** Elite Homeowners Working with Global Interior Architects (Net Worth > ₹40 Cr+)
- **Core Purchase Triggers:** Thermoformed curved island counters, seamless Corian, zero-linear edge design.

---

## 1. Corporate Identity & Manufacturing Footprint
- **Corporate Ownership:** Pedini S.p.A. (Pioneer of curved kitchen design and ergonomic modular concepts since 1957).
- **Country of Origin:** Italy (Lucrezia di Cartoceto, Pesaro)
- **Indian Footprint & Experience Centres:** No permanent subsidiary in India. Imported through bespoke high-end architectural sourcing and boutique design consultants.
- **Manufacturing Facilities:** 100% manufactured in Lucrezia di Cartoceto, Marche, Italy.
- **Quality & Environmental Certifications:** ISO 9001/14001, OHSAS 18001, Forest Stewardship Council (FSC).

---

## 2. Product Benchmarking: Substrates, Hardware & Warranty
- **Carcass Core Material:** Curved thermoformed marine-grade composite board and Idroleb water-resistant ecological particleboard.
- **Shutter Finishes Offered:** Corian seamless thermoformed surfaces, natural oxidized wood veneers, liquid metals, micalised lacquers.
- **Hardware Partner & Systems:** Custom Grass and Blum concealed runner systems engineered for curved drawer geometry.
- **Manufacturer Warranty:** 5-Year International Factory Warranty.

## 3. Go-to-Market Channels, Lead Times & Service SLA
- **Distribution Channels:** Architectural import contracts, high-net-worth individual commissions, international interior procurement agencies.
