# HomeLane — Company Profile & Strategic Intelligence Dossier

## Target Customer Classification
- **Category:** **Middle Class to Upper Middle Class**
- **Market Tier:** First-Time Homeowners & Tech Professionals
- **Target Home Value:** ₹45 Lakhs to ₹1.5 Crore (First-Time Apartment Owners)
- **Income & Wealth Profile:** Dual-income IT & Corporate Couples (Annual Income: ₹18L – ₹40L)
- **Core Purchase Triggers:** Time-sensitive, demands strict move-in deadlines, digital 3D transparency, and EMI financing.

## 1. Corporate Identity & Manufacturing Footprint
- **Corporate Ownership:** HomeLane D2C Private Limited (Well-funded Indian interior tech pioneer, backed by Peak XV, Accel, and Stride Ventures; acquired Design Cafe).
- **Country of Origin:** India (Bengaluru, Karnataka)
- **Indian Footprint & Experience Centres:** 50+ experience centres in 25+ cities including Bengaluru, Mumbai, Delhi-NCR, Hyderabad, Chennai, Pune, and Kolkata.
- **Manufacturing Facilities:** Distributed Industry 4.0 factory network and automated assembly hubs in Bengaluru, Bhiwandi, and Manesar.
- **Quality & Environmental Certifications:** CARB-2 emission certified boards, ISO 9001:2015, E1 certified adhesives.

- **Carcass Core Material:** IS 710 BWP Plywood, BWR Plywood, and High Density Moisture Resistant (HDHMR) board.
