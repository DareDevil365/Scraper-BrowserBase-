## 2. Complete Pricing Architecture & Pricebook Breakdown

### A. Base Project Costs & Range
* **Minimum Base Starting Price:** **₹1,90,000 (Essential modular kitchen package)**
* **Typical Project Range (Mid to High):** **₹2,50,000 – ₹5,50,000 (Full kitchen with tandem drawers and tall unit)**

### B. Indicative Rate Per Square Foot (Cabinetry & Shutters)
- **Laminate on Commercial / MDF:** ₹1,300 – ₹1,900 / sq. ft.
- **Anti-scratch Acrylic on HDHMR:** ₹2,000 – ₹2,900 / sq. ft.
- **Super-Matte / PU Finish:** ₹3,000 – ₹4,500 / sq. ft.
- **Tinted Glass & Veneer Accents:** ₹4,500 – ₹6,500 / sq. ft.

### C. Package Inclusions vs. Exclusions
* **Standard Inclusions:** Modular carcass, shutters, Hettich/Ebco hardware, handles, skirting, 3D design render via SpaceCraft.
* **Typical Exclusions (Add-on Costs):** Countertop stone, appliances, tiling/electrical civil modifications.
