## 1. Company Snapshot & Manufacturing Footprint
* **Corporate Ownership:** Panasonic Life Solutions India Pvt. Ltd. (formerly Anchor Electricals, 100% subsidiary of Panasonic Holdings Corporation, Japan).
* **Indian Presence & Channel Footprint:** 40+ Kitchen Experience Centres and franchise studios across Tier 1 and Tier 2 cities in India.
* **Manufacturing Facilities:** Combination of precision hardware and smart components imported from Japan, with panel fabrication in Daman and Haridwar.
* **Quality & Environmental Certifications:** Japanese Industrial Standards (JIS) four-star F-Zero formaldehyde emission, ISO 9001/14001.

## 2. Complete Pricing Architecture & Pricebook Breakdown

### A. Base Project Costs & Range
* **Minimum Base Starting Price:** **₹1,80,000 (I-Class modular starter kitchen)**
* **Typical Project Range (Mid to High):** **₹3,00,000 – ₹10,00,000 (L-Class luxury customized setup)**
- **Tiering Architecture:** Offers two distinct series: 'I-Class' (designed for Indian cooking needs and affordable luxury) and 'L-Class' (luxury flagship with imported Japanese smart storage).

### B. Indicative Rate Per Square Foot (Cabinetry & Shutters)
- **I-Class Standard Laminate:** ₹1,600 – ₹2,200 / sq. ft.
- **I-Class High Gloss Acrylic:** ₹2,400 – ₹3,500 / sq. ft.
- **L-Class Stainless Steel / Special PU:** ₹3,800 – ₹5,500 / sq. ft.
- **L-Class High-Tech Ceramic / Wood:** ₹5,500 – ₹9,000 / sq. ft.

### C. Package Inclusions vs. Exclusions
* **Standard Inclusions:** Patented Japanese 'Magic Hopper' pull-down overhead trays, soft-close corner carousels, moisture barrier sink base.
* **Typical Exclusions (Add-on Costs):** Countertop stone, built-in induction/hob, electrical chimney.
