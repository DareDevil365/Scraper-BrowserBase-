- **Target Home Value:** ₹2.0 Crore to ₹10+ Crore (Builder Projects & Exporter Imports)

## 1. Corporate Identity & Manufacturing Footprint
- **Corporate Ownership:** Lineadecor Mutfak (Turkey's leading modular kitchen manufacturer and exporter).

- **Indian Footprint & Experience Centres:** No direct company-owned showrooms in India; operates via project developers, hospitality procurement, and Turkish export channels.

- **Quality & Environmental Certifications:** ISO 9001/14001, OHSAS 18001, TSE (Turkish Standards Institution) certified quality.
