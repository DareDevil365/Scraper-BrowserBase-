## 1. Company Snapshot & Manufacturing Footprint
* **Corporate Ownership:** Lineadecor Mutfak (One of Europe and the Middle East's largest kitchen exporters, established 1991).
* **Indian Presence & Channel Footprint:** No dedicated direct retail office in India. Sourced for large-scale real estate projects and institutional builder developments via project contractors.
* **Manufacturing Facilities:** Automated modern plant spanning 30,000 sq. meters in Gebze, Turkey.
* **Quality & Environmental Certifications:** ISO 9001:2015, ISO 14001, OHSAS 18001, Turkish Standards Institute (TSE) Gold Certificate.
