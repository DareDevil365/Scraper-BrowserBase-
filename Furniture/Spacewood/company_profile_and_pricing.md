## 1. Company Snapshot & Manufacturing Footprint
* **Corporate Ownership:** Spacewood Furnishers Pvt. Ltd. (Japan's Sumitomo Forestry holds a strategic equity stake).
* **Indian Presence & Channel Footprint:** Over 150+ brand stores and dealer networks across 60+ Indian cities; major institutional supplier to top builders and e-commerce platforms.
* **Manufacturing Facilities:** India's largest single-location modular furniture plant spanning 25 acres in Hingna, Nagpur, featuring fully automated German Homag lines.
* **Quality & Environmental Certifications:** ISO 9001/14001/OHSAS 18001, FSC Certified Wood, Green Building Council of India compliant.

## 2. Complete Pricing Architecture & Pricebook Breakdown

* **Minimum Base Starting Price:** **₹1,49,000 (Straight line modular kitchen standard package)**
* **Typical Project Range (Mid to High):** **₹2,00,000 – ₹4,50,000 (Complete modular setup for 2BHK/3BHK homes)**

### B. Indicative Rate Per Square Foot (Cabinetry & Shutters)
- **Pre-laminated Particleboard / MFC:** ₹1,100 – ₹1,600 / sq. ft.
- **High Gloss Laminate on Plywood:** ₹1,700 – ₹2,400 / sq. ft.
- **Seamless Acrylic on HDHMR:** ₹2,400 – ₹3,600 / sq. ft.
- **PU Lacquer / Membrane Finish:** ₹3,800 – ₹5,500 / sq. ft.

### C. Package Inclusions vs. Exclusions
* **Standard Inclusions:** Base units, wall units, wire baskets, soft-close hinges, skirting, handles.
* **Typical Exclusions (Add-on Costs):** Chimney, hob, sink, countertop, plumbing labor.
